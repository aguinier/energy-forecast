## 3. CH `wind_onshore`, and what this settles

The ABL-316 ledger's revision 3 §7 corrected revision 2 by pointing out that G2 is
registered on `constant_causal` (79.07%), which CH beats by +40.03%, so **CH
grades A on the ladder as it was registered**. That correction was right, and the
ABL-435 pack was right to refuse to grade CH against an oracle.

What the amendment adds is the third answer neither could give: against a
*causally available* constant at 43.93% and a causally available hour-of-day mean
at 42.00%, CH's 47.42% loses. So the substance of revision 2's claim was correct
and its evidence was not, and the pre-registered route to saying so is now on the
record rather than in a footnote. **CH is a B under the amendment.** Its 12.9 MW
gate-window mean still makes it not-decision-grade either way (ABL-348), so this
changes no promotion, only what the letter means.

## 5. Limits

- **A trailing window converges; it does not teleport.** On a worst-case step at
  the gate boundary it halves the reference's error rather than removing it. The
  residual is printed per cell as `level inflation` on *both* causal references —
  NL's goes from 205% to 22.8%, CH's from 96% to 9.0%. Do not quote the corrected
  reference as exact.
- **One seed, one 30-day summer holdout**, unchanged from every ABL-348 read.
- **The amendment cannot reach tranche 1a**, whose record predates ABL-389 and
  carries no reference column at all; `abl435-tranche2f` is the current record for
  BG and CH `wind_onshore` and is what was re-read.
- **`abl253` / `abl376` (BE, DE, FR solar) are out of scope** — a different
  registration and not in ABL-316's ledger.
- **Contamination**: this read changes no source, window or ingest, so ABL-71 /
  ABL-67 / ABL-109 / ABL-111 stand exactly as each tranche recorded them. BG
  solar's night floor and EE/FI solar's coverage shortfalls are unchanged and
  still travel with those pairs.
